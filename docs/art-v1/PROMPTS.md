# ImageGen 美术方向与生成记录

使用用户指定的 imagegen 技能，默认内置 image_gen 工具；不使用CLI或远程运行时生成。原版几何和玩法不变，素材随离线包内联。

视觉方向：劫后佛国。青绿山水构成大块远景；朱砂、残金与低饱和石色建立建筑；黑莲以暗紫与玫红危险高光表示。悟空保留猴相、红色披帛、金冠和长棍剪影，人物不使用大头方块公仔比例。落脚面清晰，装饰低于危险与角色对比。

## 01 山道环境

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: production background raster for a 2D side-scrolling pixel-art action game, 16:9 wide.
Primary request: art-direct an atmospheric ruined Buddhist mountain approach for a Sun Wukong game inspired by Journey to the West Afterstory. A once sacred realm under a black-lotus eclipse, still containing human warmth. This must look like thoughtfully authored premium pixel game environment art.
Scene/backdrop: towering blue-green Chinese karst mountains in layered turquoise haze; ancient weathered cliffside Buddhist halls and broken vermilion gateways integrated into the rock; carved sandstone lotus reliefs, a distant tarnished-gold stupa, sparse windblown red prayer ribbons and warm amber lanterns. A few dark lotus forms invade the architecture, subtle rather than busy.
Composition/framing: orthographic side-view background, wide 16:9. Strong large shapes, asymmetrical architecture with one memorable focal silhouette. Atmospheric perspective: far jade mountains, a middle layer of red-brown stone temple ruins. Preserve a broad low-contrast misty blue-green band across the bottom third so gameplay platforms and enemies can be drawn on top. No playable foreground floor, no floating platforms, no characters, no HUD. The entire image is the background, no framed presentation board.
Style/medium: authored pixel art, visible crisp pixel clusters, restrained 48-colour mineral-pigment palette, intentional edge stepping and selective dithering; detail through grouped shapes rather than noisy texture; no soft painted blur, no fake scanlines. Pixel-art depth and lighting, not vector geometry.
Lighting/mood: late dusk after catastrophe, turquoise ambient light, a few amber lanterns, faint warm rim light along ancient roofs. Solemn, mysterious, hopeful traces of civilisation. Darkest values in nearby architectural silhouettes, never a flat black void.
Text: none.
Constraints: no logos, no watermarks, no readable letters, no menu mockup, no isometric view, no perspective floor, no giant centred moon, no repetitive triangle trees, no flat rectangle towers, no modern buildings. Deliver one polished game-ready environmental image.

Result: `assets/art-v1/mountain-approach.png`, generated by built-in image_gen, source saved under Codex generated_images and copied into workspace. This image sets the visual reference for the following assets.

## 02 悟空动作图集

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production transparent sprite atlas for the same 2D pixel-art game.
Reference: mountain-approach.png supplies only the visual palette and level of pixel detail, not composition. Generate Sun Wukong as a composed, battle-experienced monkey warrior: visible simian face and muzzle, gold circlet with two restrained red feather tips, russet fur, layered tarnished-gold lamellar shoulder armour over dark teal travel robes, vermilion scarf and waist cloth, worn boots, long iron staff with gold bands. Slender athletic silhouette, approximately three heads tall, never a square toy or cute mascot.
Exact atlas layout: 4 columns by 2 rows of equally sized cells. One complete full-body sprite per cell, same scale and bottom-centre baseline, facing RIGHT in every cell. Generous transparent gutter and staff margin in each cell; nothing crosses a cell boundary. Canvas is wide 3:2. Reading order: idle with staff upright; run stride leaning forward; jump rising with tucked legs; fall with legs ready to land; windup drawing staff back; horizontal staff strike with full body twist; recovery drawing staff inward; hurt recoil. No repeated front-facing portraits.
Authored pixel art with crisp clusters and readable silhouette, matching the environment's mineral-pigment colour language. Charcoal outline, grouped highlights, warm gold and vermilion pop against teal. No scene, floor, shadows cast on a background, text, labels, grid lines or checkerboard. Genuine transparent background. Preserve character identity, costume proportions and staff design across all eight frames. Deliver the full atlas as one image.

Draft: `wukong-atlas-draft.png`. Metadata confirms genuine alpha (929909 fully transparent pixels; sampled corners alpha=0). The image preview exposes RGB under alpha, not an actual opaque backdrop. Layout is not yet approved: the strike's staff crosses the cell boundary. Targeted edit: keep character identity and eight poses, re-pack into 4×2 square slots on a wide 2:1 sheet, reduce all sprites by one shared scale, align feet to each cell's baseline, leave at least 10% transparent padding, no staff or scarf crosses its cell. Preserve genuine alpha, no text or drawn grid.

Packed result: `assets/art-v1/wukong-atlas.png`. Atlas is genuine RGBA. Runtime uses explicit frame rectangles and foot anchors; the long strike extends into the unused gutter, so naïve equal-grid slicing is deliberately not used. All art is preserved and no background-colour keying is applied.

## Integration

Six selected PNGs are embedded by `tools/pack-art.cjs` into `src/generated-art.js`; source assets remain in the repository. No runtime fetch/CDN/API. The discarded first Wukong layout stays as an iteration record and is not packed. Source frame rectangles and anchors are in `src/visuals.js`; nonuniform atlas gutters are handled explicitly rather than cutting weapons. Title uses the source sprite at larger resolution; runtime uses cached frames. UI colours/materials align to the same aged bronze, blue-green stone and vermilion system.
