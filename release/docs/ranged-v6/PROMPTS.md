首次完整图集提示词：

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production sprite animation strip for an existing Chinese dark-fantasy pixel side-view platform action game. Create ONE transparent PNG strip with EXACTLY SIX equal-width horizontal slots in a 3:1 wide canvas, all six sprites separated with generous empty gutters and no overlap. Subject in EVERY slot is the SAME Nezha, a young Chinese mythic male deity, black hair in two small buns with short red ties, expressive determined face, ivory and jade-green lotus armour with muted antique gold fittings, long flowing crimson red Huntian silk ribbon, dark teal trousers, red boots. He is distinct from a monkey warrior. All poses face RIGHT, side profile / slight three-quarter view, identical body proportions and readable silhouette. A medium-sized gold ring (Qiankun ring) is his main weapon. Slot order left to right: 1 idle holding ring beside shoulder; 2 run stride with ribbon swept back; 3 airborne jump with knees tucked; 4 deliberate throwing anticipation with ring behind right shoulder; 5 release follow-through with forward arm extended and EMPTY hand (the game renders the flying ring separately); 6 hurt/recoil, still upright, ring close to body. Every entire character and all ribbons must stay INSIDE its slot, feet baseline aligned and consistent scale. Intended runtime body height about 70 pixels in a 960x540 game; sprite must read at small scale. Match rich but CRISP classic pixel clusters, strong stepped outline, limited amber/ivory/jade/red palette, cel-like three-tone shading and subtle metallic highlights. No smooth painting, no blur, no bloom. Genuine transparent background, no floor, no shadows beyond sprite, no scenery, no labels, no text, no watermark, no poster composition. This is a usable sprite atlas, not a concept sheet.
